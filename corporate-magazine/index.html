12Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V.

Company overview

Who we are

Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V. operates where environmental management and energy markets meet. Its business starts from hydrocarbons that already exist and that, because of their origin, condition or location, require technical, environmental and documentary management.

The company does not participate in the exploration or primary production of crude oil. It recovers technically usable hydrocarbons under the applicable authorizations, treats and conditions them, establishes their characteristics and regulatory status and, only then, connects them with qualified energy markets.

Its operating base is Mexico, under Mexican energy, environmental and commercial law.

Our story

Origin

Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V. was born from identifying a gap in the Mexican energy industry: there were volumes of remnant crude that required environmental remediation, with no specialized player to treat them with the technical and documentary rigor that the international market demands.

Problem

The Mexican oil industry, with more than a century of activity, has left remnant crude and impregnated soils at various points of the territory. They are an environmental risk if left unaddressed, and an untapped energy resource if they are not recovered through the right process.

Solution

Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V. develops a model of environmental crude recovery: identification, assessment, recovery, treatment, conditioning, traceability and, as a consequence of that process, commercialization, under verifiable regulatory compliance.

Future

Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V. aims to consolidate environmental crude recovery as a recognized standard in Mexico, expanding its technical capacity and its network of international counterparties under the same principle: evidence before promise.

15Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V.

Manifesto

There is a space in the energy industry that almost nobody takes seriously: that of the hydrocarbon that was left behind. The remnant crude, the impregnated soil, the volume that no ordinary operation claims but that the environment demands be treated. That space is no minor problem. It is, at once, a risk if ignored and a resource if addressed with the technical rigor it deserves.

Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V. exists to occupy exactly that space. We do not compete in primary crude extraction —that is the task of the Mexican State. We do not compete to be the largest trader or the fastest broker. We compete to be, in our territory, the most rigorous: in identifying the resource, in recovering it, in treating it, in its documentary traceability and, only then, in its commercialization.

We believe commercialization should not be the starting point of a company that works with sensitive hydrocarbons; it should be its consequence. A consequence of having recovered responsibly. A consequence of having treated in accordance with the standard. A consequence of having documented every stage until it can be verified by any third party —a bank, a refinery, an authority— without having to take our word as the only evidence.

We operate under a simple, non-negotiable principle: evidence before promise. If something cannot be supported by a document, a certificate or an authorization, we do not communicate it as fact. We prefer to say “this is pending verification” rather than say something we cannot sustain under serious analysis.

We are not an environmental company that makes oil a side business, nor an oil company that uses environmental language as cover. We are something more specific and, we believe, more valuable: a company that has made environmental crude recovery its core discipline, with commercialization as the natural result of doing that work well.

We want to be, for whoever evaluates us —bank, refinery, authority, investor, community— the company that answers with documents before adjectives. The one that says “pending confirmation” when appropriate, instead of filling the gap with an attractive figure. The one that understands that its long-term reputation is worth more than any short-term operation.

This is Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V.: recovery with purpose, commercialization as consequence, and evidence as principle.

What we do

Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V. is a Mexican company specialized in environmental remediation and in the treatment, conditioning and valorization of hydrocarbons, integrating technical, operational, regulatory and logistical capabilities.

Our model lets us recover technically usable hydrocarbons from processes subject to environmental management, transforming them through treatment and conditioning into energy resources with commercial value, in particular crude oil.

Commercialization is not the starting point of Corporación Richardson, S.A. de C.V.; it is the natural result of having recovered, treated and conditioned a hydrocarbon to a marketable condition. This distinction matters: the company does not take part in primary crude oil extraction —an activity constitutionally reserved to the Mexican State through Petróleos Mexicanos (Pemex)— but in the recovery, treatment and commercialization of existing hydrocarbons that require remediation, under the corresponding permits and authorizations.

Subject to applicable authorizations and/or authorized specialized operators.
